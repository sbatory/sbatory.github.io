GymDue - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

GymDue tracks the time you spend exercising against a weekly goal. It is designed to work entirely on your device: there are no user accounts, no sign-in, and we operate no servers that receive your data.

Data you enter stays on your device

Everything you put into GymDue - your workouts and their times, durations and notes, the workout types you define, your weekly goal and settings, and any adjustments you make to your carried balance - is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local storage. We cannot see it. It leaves your device only when you choose to export it, and that file goes wherever you send it.

The app makes no network requests

GymDue contains no networking code at all. It never contacts any server - not ours, not anyone's - so there is no moment at which your data, or even the fact that you opened the app, is transmitted anywhere. Nothing to send, and nowhere to send it.

Apple Health

GymDue does not request access to Apple Health. It neither reads your health or fitness data nor writes workouts back to it. The workouts in GymDue exist only inside GymDue.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No third-party SDKs or frameworks of any kind - the app uses only Apple's own
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or health permissions - the app requests none
  • No selling or sharing of data - we never possess it in the first place

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, that backup includes GymDue's data along with everything else on the device. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's terms, not by us, and we have no access to them.

TestFlight betas

If you use a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share standard beta feedback and crash logs with us as described in Apple's privacy policy. These contain diagnostic information, not the workouts you record in the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete any individual workout from the Calendar tab, and remove workout types in Settings. To remove everything at once, delete the app - all of its stored data goes with it.
