SlugMenu — Support

Last updated: September 15, 2026

SlugMenu shows UC Santa Cruz dining hall and cafe menus, hours and nutrition information. If something is wrong or missing, please get in touch.

Contact

Email julek@batory.pl. Please say which hall and which day, and include a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

A menu looks wrong. Menus come from UCSC Dining and the app republishes them. UCSC sometimes changes a menu after publishing it, so the app re-checks today's menu every hour. If it still looks wrong after an hour, the source itself has not been updated.

A hall shows as closed when it is open. Hours come from UCSC Dining's published schedule. Holiday and quarter-break hours change often. Please report it with the hall name and time.

Allergen and dietary filters. The app hides dishes tagged with allergens you select. Those tags are what UCSC posts about a dish as normally prepared. They do not cover substitutions, cross-contact or last-minute changes in the kitchen. If you have a serious allergy, always confirm with dining staff before eating.

Deleting your data. Everything the app stores stays on your device. Delete the app and it goes with it.
